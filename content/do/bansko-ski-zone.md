The ski zone sits on the slopes of the Pirin mountains above the town. An 8-person gondola takes you from the lower station in Bansko to Banderishka Polyana, the base of the ski area, in about 25 minutes. From there, chairlifts spread out towards Todorka peak, with the highest runs at around 2,600 m.

## What to expect

- About 75 km of marked pistes across three linked areas: Banderishka Polyana, Chalin Valog and Shiligarnika. There are runs for every level, from wide nursery slopes to steep blacks under Todorka.
- The ski road, a long gentle run back to town, is lit for night skiing once a week.
- Ski schools, equipment hire and lockers are at the gondola station and at Banderishka Polyana.

## Season

The season usually runs from mid-December to mid-April, depending on snow. In summer the gondola normally runs for hikers heading up to Vihren hut and the Banderishki lakes. Check the official site for current dates, prices and lift status.

## Tips

- The gondola queue is longest between 08:30 and 09:30 on weekends and holidays. Go a little later, or buy your lift pass online the day before.
- Ski buses from town stop at the gondola. Parking at the station fills up early on busy days.
