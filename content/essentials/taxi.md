## Airport transfers

Sofia Airport is about 160 km away, around 2.5 hours by car. Book transfers a day ahead in winter.

- **Transfer company** – phone or website

## Ski bus

In season a ski bus runs between town and the gondola. Ask security for the nearest stop and the timetable.
